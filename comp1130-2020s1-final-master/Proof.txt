Just (helper x xs)  = rLast (x:xs) 

8 ii) Put your answer here:

-- Base Case #1 a.k.a. BC1
Just (helper x []) = rLast (x:[])

-- Base Case #2 a.k.a. BC2
Just (helper x (y:[])) = rLast (x:y:[])

8 iii) Put your answer here:

-- Proof of BC1
Just (helper x [])
= Just x           -- (H1)
= rLast [x]        -- (R2)
= rLast (x:[])

-- Proof of BC2
Just (helper x (y:[]))
= Just (helper y [])    -- (H2)
= rLast (y:[])          -- (BC1)
= rLast (x:y:[])        -- (R3)

8 iv) Put your answer here:

for all x: Just (helper x xs)  = rLast (x:xs)

8 v) Put your answer here:

Just (helper x (y:xs))  = rLast (x:y:xs)

8 vi) Put your answer here:

rLast (x:y:xs)
= rLast (y:xs)           -- (R3)
= Just (helper y xs)     -- (IH)
= Just (helper x (y:xs)) -- (H2)

8 vii) Put your answer here:

-- Proof of empty case
lLast []
= Nothing   -- (L1)
= rLast []  -- (R1)

-- Proof of non-empty case
lLast (x:xs)
= Just (helper x xs)   -- (L2)
= rLast (x:xs)         -- (Lemma1)

Therefore, by extensionality, if (for all xs: lLast xs = rLast xs) then lLast = rLast.
